Cross-Team Retro Guide

How to run a retrospective across several teams that finds and fixes problems no single team can solve.

“What got in the way between teams, not within them?”

Team retros improve how each team works, but many problems live between teams: hand-offs, dependencies, shared environments and unclear decisions. A cross-team retro, held at the end of a planning period, brings representatives from every team together to review the shared objectives, collect what went well and what didn't across teams, and solve the biggest system problem in mixed-team groups. SAFe calls this the Inspect & Adapt event's problem-solving workshop.

1Before the session

  • Print the board at A1 or A3 (or 11×17), or use a digital board with breakout areas
  • Bring the shared objectives and their results from the Cross-Team Planning board
  • Ask each team to bring the top cross-team themes from their own retros
  • Bring the Dependency Map and the Impediments boards
  • Green, pink and blue stickies, dot stickers and markers

3Making each part strong

Quality checks for the board.

1 · What We Set Out to Do

  • Data, not opinions
  • Learning, not blame

2 · Went Well Across Teams

  • Specific examples
  • Worth repeating

3 · Cross-Team Problems

  • Between teams, not within
  • Dot-voted

4 · Ideas

  • Anyone can contribute
  • Small and testable

5 · Solve the Top Problem

  • Mixed-team groups
  • A root cause, not a symptom

6 · Follow Through

  • Improvements in the next plan
  • Each has an owner

4Facilitation tips

Focus between teamsSteer team-internal issues back to team retros. This session is for the system.
Mix the groupsProblem-solving groups with people from several teams see the whole picture.
One problem, solvedFixing one system problem well beats a long list of half-fixes.
Leaders as participantsLeaders can remove system constraints that teams can't. Invite them to help solve.
Plan it inPut improvements into the next planning period's capacity, or they won't happen.
Close the loopStart the next cross-team retro by reviewing the last one's improvements.
Common pitfalls: teams airing internal issues; blaming other teams; too many improvements; no owners; improvements not planned into the next period; skipping it when a period goes well.

5After the session

Straight away

Share the improvements and owners with all teams.

Next planning

Plan the improvements into the Cross-Team Planning board's capacity.

During the period

Track progress at the Cross-Team Sync.

Next retro

Review the improvements first: did they help?

6How it fits the toolkit

The program tools coordinate several teams: agree Objectives & Key Results, plan together with the Cross-Team Planning board, make links visible on the Dependency Map, and keep in step with a regular Cross-Team Sync. Each team still plans, reviews and improves with the team-level tools.

Create an editable whiteboard for “Cross-Team Retro”, a workshop board.

Purpose: Reflect across teams at the end of a period: what worked, what didn't, and one system problem to fix together.

Layout: a wide landscape board (about 16:11). Make one frame per section below, placed as described. In each frame, add the section name as a heading, then its content as short text labels or empty fields to fill in. Leave space in each frame for sticky notes. Use the header colour given. Keep the wording as written and don't add extra content.
Key: Green = Went well · Pink = Problem · Blue = Idea · Dot = Vote

Sections:
1. What We Set Out to Do: top full width, about 100% wide and 15% tall; header colour slate grey.
   Subtitle: Bring the shared objectives and the data
   Content: Shared objective: · Planned value: · Achieved: · What we learned:
2. Went Well Across Teams: middle left, about 35% wide and 45% tall; header colour emerald green.
   Subtitle: Keep doing
   Content: Where did teams help each other?
3. Cross-Team Problems: middle centre, about 35% wide and 45% tall; header colour red.
   Subtitle: Dot-vote the biggest
   Content: Where did work wait between teams?
4. Ideas: middle right, about 35% wide and 45% tall; header colour blue.
   Subtitle: For any team to try
   Content: What would make next period smoother?
5. Solve the Top Problem: bottom left, about 65% wide and 35% tall; header colour purple.
   Subtitle: In mixed-team groups
   Content: Top problem (most dots): · Improvement: · Teams involved: · Owner: · Why 1? · Why 2? · Why 3? · Why 4? · Root cause:
6. Follow Through: bottom right, about 35% wide and 35% tall; header colour dark green.
   Subtitle: Into the next planning
   Content: Improvements added to the next cross-team plan · Each improvement has an owner · Teams' own retros have the same themes? · Progress checked at the cross-team sync · We'll measure success by: · Next cross-team retro:

Footer questions: “What got in the way between teams, not within them?” “What one change would help every team next period?”
