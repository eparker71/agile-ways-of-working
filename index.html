Agile Ways of Working Toolkit

Lean, agile and hybrid tools for teams, facilitators and leaders.

Printable boards (A3 or 11×17), posters and facilitator guides, grouped by what you're trying to do. Open a board to view or print it, drop the SVG into Miro, Mural or FigJam, or download a guide in Word to adapt it. Every guide also has a ready-made AI prompt that rebuilds its board as an editable whiteboard in Miro AI, Atlassian Rovo or similar.
