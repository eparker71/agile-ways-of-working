The toolkit

What it is, and what it isn't

  • Free and open. Everything is free to use, adapt and share, with credit, under CC BY 4.0. See Make It Yours for how.
  • Framework-neutral. The tools come from many sources, including Scrum, Kanban, XP, Lean and scaled frameworks. A framework is named only as the source of a practice, and the people whose ideas a tool is based on are credited on it.
  • Made to be used. Each board prints at A3 or 11×17, or works on a digital whiteboard, and comes with a facilitator guide, an editable Word version and a prompt for recreating it with AI.
  • Built with AI. I've been building personal apps with generative AI tools such as Claude Code, and this toolkit is one of them: I designed and wrote it, working with AI to generate the boards, guides and checks.
  • A personal project. It isn't affiliated with, or endorsed by, my employer or any certification or framework body. Trademarks belong to their owners.
