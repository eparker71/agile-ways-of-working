Kanban Cadences Guide

How to set up a rhythm of short, focused reviews that keep work flowing and services improving.

“Which conversation would most improve our flow if we had it regularly?”

Teams working in continuous flow don't need iterations, but they still need feedback loops. The Kanban Method (David J. Anderson) describes a set of cadences: a daily Kanban meeting, replenishment to choose what to pull in next, delivery planning, a service delivery review of flow and service levels, and wider operations, risk and strategy reviews. You don't need them all. Start with the daily meeting, replenishment and a service delivery review, then add others as the system grows.

3The main cadences

Start with the first three; add others as you grow.

Daily Kanban Meeting

  • Walk the board right to left
  • Blockers and ageing work

Replenishment

  • Pull to fill free WIP
  • Clear selection policy

Service Delivery Review

  • Flow against expectations
  • Customers or PO present

Delivery Planning

  • What's ready to release, and when

Operations · Risk · Strategy

  • Wider, less frequent
  • For several services or teams

4Facilitation tips

Start smallThree cadences done well beat seven done half-heartedly.
Decouple the cadencesReplenishment, delivery and review can each run at their own pace, unlike a single iteration.
Pull, don't pushReplenish only to fill free WIP. Never overload the system to look busy.
Bring dataService delivery reviews work best with lead time and SLE data, not opinions.
Invite the customerHearing expectations directly keeps the service focused on what matters.
Review the rhythmEvery few months, ask whether each cadence is still worth its time.
Common pitfalls: copying every cadence at once; replenishing beyond WIP limits; reviews without data; no customer voice; keeping meetings that no longer help.

5After the session

Straight away

Book the cadences as recurring calendar events.

First month

Hold each at least once, and adjust timing and attendees.

Every quarter

Review the rhythm: add, drop or change a cadence.

Always

Bring service delivery review findings to the retrospective.
