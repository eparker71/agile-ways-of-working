“Who haven't we paired with yet, and what could we learn from each other?”

Pairing means two people working on one thing at one screen; mobbing, or ensemble work, means the whole group does. Both finish work with fewer hand-offs, catch problems early, and spread skills quickly. They work for code, tests, analysis and design. The board covers four styles, a pair stair to track who has paired with whom, session agreements, the skills each pairing should spread, and a quick retro. Pair programming comes from Extreme Programming; strong-style pairing is from Llewellyn Falco; mob programming was popularised by Woody Zuill.

3Choosing a style

Each style suits different work. Swap styles as the work changes.

Driver / Navigator

  • Everyday pairing
  • Swap every 15–30 minutes

Ping-Pong

  • Test-driven development
  • Keeps both people engaged

Strong-Style

  • Teaching and onboarding
  • The expert navigates, the learner drives

Mob / Ensemble

  • Hard problems, swarming, new areas
  • Rotate the driver every few minutes

2 · Pair Stair

  • Spread tallies evenly
  • Empty cells are pairs to try

4Facilitation tips

Start with a trialAgree to pair for two weeks, then review. People adjust faster when it's an experiment.
Swap oftenRegular role swaps keep both people engaged and stop one person dominating.
Breaks matterPairing is intense. Short, regular breaks keep it sustainable.
Strong-style to teachLet the learner drive and the expert navigate. Knowledge moves through the learner's hands.
Mob for hard problemsWhen something is stuck or new, mobbing brings every perspective at once.
Not everything needs a pairAgree what's fine solo: simple, well-understood or exploratory work.
Common pitfalls: one person typing while the other watches; the same pairs every time; no breaks; forcing pairing on everything without agreement; measuring individual output; experts never letting learners drive.

5After the session

Straight away

Put the agreements and the pair stair next to the team board.

Daily

Plan pairs at the daily sync, using the stair to mix people.

Weekly

Run the quick retro and update the tallies.

Quarterly

Re-score the Skills Matrix. Pairing should show up as levels going up.
