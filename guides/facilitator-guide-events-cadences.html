“Does every event we hold have a clear purpose and output?”

Frameworks name their events differently, but the needs underneath are the same: plan, sync, refine, review, reflect, and do the same across teams and for the organisation. The poster lists twelve common events with what each framework calls them, their purpose, typical cadence and timebox, who comes, the output, and the tool in this kit that helps. Use it to design a lean set of events for a team or a group of teams, or to translate between frameworks. Timeboxes are typical; Scrum's are maximums for each week of a sprint.

1Before the session

  • Print the poster at A3 or 11×17, or share it on screen
  • List the meetings the team or group holds today, with their length and frequency
  • Bring the team's working agreements or charter
  • Invite the people who'd attend the events

3Reading the poster

How the three groups fit together.

Team events

  • Plan, sync, refine, review, reflect
  • Weekly to daily rhythm

Multi-team events

  • The same needs across teams
  • Period or quarterly rhythm

Organisation events

  • Funding and outcomes
  • Monthly or quarterly

Also called

  • Translate between frameworks
  • Pick names your people use

4Facilitation tips

Purpose firstAn event without a clear purpose and output is a candidate to drop.
Fewer, betterA lean set of well-run events beats a full calendar.
Same needs, different namesUse the “also called” column to stop arguments about terminology.
Timebox everythingShorter, regular events are easier to keep focused than long, rare ones.
Scale the timeboxScrum's timeboxes scale with sprint length; a two-week sprint needs half the time of a four-week one.
Review the setEvery quarter, ask which events still earn their time.
Common pitfalls: adding every event from every framework; events with no output; long meetings with the wrong people; arguing about names instead of purpose; never reviewing the calendar.

5After the session

First month

6How it fits the toolkit

The event tools follow a team's rhythm: Sprint Planning or replenishment, the Daily Sync, Backlog Refinement, Sprint Review and a retrospective from the Retro Kit. Teams working in continuous flow use the Kanban Cadences. The Events & Cadences poster shows them all, including multi-team and leadership events.
