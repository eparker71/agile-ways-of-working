# Start well

## New team launch day
For: a team that's just formed, or has changed so much it's effectively new.
Time: 1 day (about 7 hours with lunch and breaks)
Outcome: a shared purpose, working agreements, a first backlog and a plan for the first iteration.
Before:
- Agree the team's mission with the sponsor, in one or two sentences, so the day starts from something.
- Print the boards at A3, or set them up as backgrounds on a digital whiteboard.
- Invite the product owner and anyone the team depends on for the backlog session.
Agenda:
- 20 min | - | Welcome, why we're here, and a personal check-in: "what I hope this team becomes".
- 75 min | Team Charter | Purpose, members and roles, values, and working agreements.
- 45 min | Skills Matrix | What we can do today, and where we'll need to learn or pair.
- 15 min | - | Break
- 60 min | Product Vision Board | Who it's for, the problem, and how we'll know it's working.
- 60 min | - | Lunch
- 75 min | User Story Map | The journey end to end, and a first thin slice.
- 15 min | - | Break
- 45 min | Team Launch (Sprint Zero) | Ways of working, environments and the first iteration's goal.
- 15 min | - | Close: one word each, and agree who writes up and shares the boards.
After:
- Photograph or export every board and share it in the team channel the same day.
- Review the charter at the first retrospective: what did we agree that isn't happening?

## Project kickoff (half day)
For: the start of a project with a fixed scope, budget or date, with the team and key stakeholders.
Time: 3½ hours
Outcome: an agreed purpose and scope, the people who matter, the biggest risks and the first plan.
Before:
- Draft the project's purpose and constraints with the sponsor.
- Make a first list of stakeholders to bring to the session.
Agenda:
- 15 min | - | Welcome, and the sponsor explains why this project, why now.
- 60 min | Project Kickoff | Purpose, scope in and out, constraints, and how we'll work.
- 40 min | Stakeholder Map | Who's affected, who decides, and how we'll keep each group involved.
- 15 min | - | Break
- 45 min | Pre-mortem | Imagine it failed: why? Turn the top reasons into actions.
- 25 min | RAID Log | Record the risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies we found.
- 10 min | - | Close: decisions made, owners, and the date of the first review.
After:
- Share the RAID log and agree how often it's reviewed.
- Send stakeholders a one-page summary using the Outcome Status Report format.

# Plan

## Quarterly planning day
For: a team, or a few teams, setting goals and plans for the next quarter.
Time: 1 day (about 6¾ hours with lunch and breaks)
Outcome: a few objectives with key results, a prioritised list, known dependencies and a confidence vote.
Before:
- Gather last quarter's results: what moved, what didn't, and what we learned.
- Ask leaders for the strategic priorities the plan should support.
- Pre-score the larger items for value and size, so the day isn't spent estimating.
Agenda:
- 30 min | Value Metrics | Review last quarter's outcomes and what they tell us.
- 75 min | Objectives & Key Results | Draft two or three objectives, each with measurable key results.
- 15 min | - | Break
- 75 min | Prioritisation Board | Cost of delay and size for each candidate; agree Now, Next and Later.
- 60 min | - | Lunch
- 60 min | Dependency Map | Who we need, who needs us, and by when.
- 15 min | - | Break
- 45 min | Pre-mortem | What could stop us? Agree the actions that make the plan safer.
- 30 min | - | Confidence vote (fist of five), then adjust the plan until most people are at 3 or more.
After:
- Publish the objectives and the Now · Next · Later roadmap where everyone can see them.
- Put a short OKR check-in on the calendar every two weeks.

## Shape a big idea
For: a product trio or small team turning a large, fuzzy request into small, testable pieces of work.
Time: 3 hours
Outcome: a clear goal, the options for reaching it, and the first stories with examples.
Before:
- Bring any research, data or customer quotes you have about the problem.
Agenda:
- 40 min | Impact Map | Goal, the people whose behaviour must change, and how.
- 40 min | Opportunity Solution Tree | Opportunities under the outcome, and a few solutions for the best one.
- 15 min | - | Break
- 45 min | Epic Breakdown Canvas | Turn the chosen solution into an epic with a hypothesis and a first slice.
- 40 min | Example Mapping | Rules, examples and questions for the first two or three stories.
After:
- Add the stories to the backlog, with their examples, ready for refinement.
- Decide what you'll measure to know whether the epic worked.

# Improve

## Reset a struggling team
For: a team where delivery has slowed, frustration is high or people have stopped speaking up.
Time: 3 hours, ideally away from the usual meeting room
Outcome: an honest shared picture, the few things that matter most, and owned experiments to try.
Before:
- Talk one-to-one with a few team members first, so nothing in the room is a surprise to you.
- Be clear with leaders that the session is the team's, and what will be shared afterwards.
Agenda:
- 20 min | - | Check-in, and agree how we'll treat each other today.
- 30 min | Psychological Safety Check | Anonymous scores first, then talk about the pattern, not individuals.
- 40 min | Team Health Check | Where we're strong, where we're struggling, and the trend.
- 15 min | - | Break
- 45 min | Root Cause Analysis | Take the top one or two problems and ask why until you reach something you can change.
- 30 min | 1% Experiment Canvas | One or two small experiments, each with an owner and a check-in date.
After:
- Check the experiments at the next two retrospectives, and celebrate small wins.
- Repeat the health check in six to eight weeks to see whether it has moved.

## Flow tune-up
For: a team whose work takes too long, gets stuck or piles up half-finished.
Time: 2½ hours
Outcome: a board that shows the real flow, sensible WIP limits, and a plan for the worst blockers.
Before:
- Export the dates each item entered and left each column over the last two or three months, if you can.
Agenda:
- 45 min | Value Stream Map | From request to customer: steps, wait times and hand-offs.
- 40 min | Blocker Clustering | Group recent blockers to find the few causes behind most of them.
- 15 min | - | Break
- 30 min | Kanban Board Design | Redesign the columns to match the real flow, and set WIP limits.
- 20 min | Ageing WIP & SLE | Agree a service level expectation and how you'll spot items going stale.
After:
- Try the new board for two weeks, then review it at the retrospective.
- Start walking the board right to left in the daily sync, using Buffering & Swarming when items stall.

## After an incident
For: the team and anyone else involved, within a week of a significant incident.
Time: 2 hours
Outcome: a shared timeline, the contributing factors, and actions that make a repeat less likely.
Before:
- Collect the timeline from logs, chat and alerts, and share it before the session.
- Remind everyone the review is blameless: we look at the system, not at who did what.
Agenda:
- 10 min | - | Set the tone: why the review is blameless, and what we'll do with the output.
- 60 min | Blameless Incident Review | Timeline, what helped, what got in the way, and contributing factors.
- 30 min | Root Cause Analysis | Dig into the most important factor.
- 20 min | - | Agree actions, owners and dates; record them in the team's backlog.
After:
- Share a summary with the wider organisation, so others can learn from it too.
- Check the actions are done at the next retrospective.

# Lead change

## Leadership offsite
For: a leadership team agreeing how it will support its teams over the next year.
Time: 1 day (about 6½ hours with lunch and breaks)
Outcome: shared priorities, clear decision rights, and a plan for the change the organisation needs.
Before:
- Ask each leader for the one thing that would most help their teams this year.
- Bring the current portfolio: what's in flight, and how money and people are split.
Agenda:
- 30 min | Leading for Psychological Safety | How leaders' behaviour shapes what teams feel able to say.
- 75 min | Portfolio Kanban & Investment Mix | What we're funding, what we should stop, and the mix we want.
- 15 min | - | Break
- 60 min | Team Topology Design | How teams are organised around the work, and where hand-offs hurt.
- 60 min | - | Lunch
- 60 min | Delegation Board | Which decisions sit where, and what we'll hand to teams.
- 15 min | - | Break
- 60 min | ADKAR Change Readiness | For the biggest change: awareness, desire, knowledge, ability, reinforcement.
- 15 min | - | Close: the three commitments we'll make to our teams.
After:
- Share the commitments with teams, in person, within a week.
- Review delegation and the investment mix every quarter.
