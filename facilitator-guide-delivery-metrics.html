“Are we getting faster without getting less stable?”

The four key metrics from the DORA research programme (DevOps Research and Assessment) balance speed (deployment frequency and lead time for changes) with stability (change failure rate and recovery time). Improving one pair at the expense of the other shows up straight away. The tech debt register makes debt visible with its interest, the ongoing cost of leaving it, and Martin Fowler's technical debt quadrant helps the team talk about why the debt exists without blame.

1Before the session

  • Print the board at A3, or load the SVG as a digital background
  • Pull data for the four metrics from your pipeline, release and incident tools, if you can
  • Agree definitions: what counts as a deployment, a failure and a recovery
  • Ask the team to list known technical debt in advance
  • Bring the Blocker Clustering and Ageing WIP boards if you use them

3Reading the metrics

Look at the four together; they're designed to balance each other.

Deployment Frequency

  • More often, smaller releases
  • Lower risk per release

Lead Time for Changes

  • Commit to production
  • Look for waiting time

Change Failure Rate

  • % of releases needing a fix
  • Rises if speed outruns quality

Recovery Time

  • How fast you restore service
  • Practice and automation help

Tech Debt Register

  • Interest, not just size
  • Owned and prioritised

4Facilitation tips

Trends, not league tablesCompare a team with its own past, never with other teams.
Balance speed and stabilityIf one pair improves while the other worsens, slow down and find out why.
Debt has interestPrioritise the debt that slows every change, not the debt that's merely ugly.
Make time explicitA fixed share of each iteration for debt beats waiting for a quiet period that never comes.
No blame in the quadrantReckless debt usually comes from pressure or missing skills. Fix the cause.
Automate the dataCollect the metrics from your tools so measuring doesn't become a chore.
Common pitfalls: using the metrics to rank teams or people; gaming deployment counts; ignoring stability; a debt list with no interest or owner; no time set aside to pay debt down; arguing about definitions forever.

5After the session

Straight away

Add the top debt items to the backlog with their interest noted.

Each iteration

Spend the agreed share on debt and enablers.

Monthly

Update the four metrics and review the trend with the team.

Quarterly

Share the trend and debt progress in the Value Metrics review (ability to innovate).

6How it fits the toolkit

The flow pack works as a set. Kanban Board Design makes the workflow visible and limits WIP. Ageing WIP & SLE shows which items are at risk and sets the swarm triggers used in Buffering & Swarming. Blocker Clustering finds why work gets stuck, and the Skills Matrix shows who can swarm. Bring the results to retrospectives and the Team Health Check.
