Lean Coffee Guide

How to run a structured, agenda-less meeting where the group decides what to talk about.

“What's the most valuable thing we could talk about right now?”

Lean Coffee replaces a fixed agenda with one the group builds on the spot. People propose topics, vote, and discuss them in priority order within short timeboxes. After each timebox the group votes to keep going or move on. It suits communities of practice, team syncs, leadership forums and open Q&A sessions.

Group4–12 people
(split if larger)

1Before the session

  • Print the board at A3 or 11×17, or load the SVG as a digital background
  • Yellow and green stickies, markers, dot stickers (3 per person)
  • A visible timer (phone, or the meeting tool's timer)
  • Decide the theme or scope, if any, and say it in the invite
  • For groups over 12, prepare two boards and split into tables

2Agenda & run-sheet

TimeActivityFacilitator notes
0–3Explain the formatWalk the five steps at the bottom of the board. Agree the timebox: 8 minutes, then +4, then +2.
3–8Brainstorm topicsSilent writing: one topic per yellow sticky in To Discuss. Topics can be questions, problems or ideas.
8–15Pitch & dot-voteEach author pitches their topic in one sentence. Group duplicates, then everyone places 3 dots. Sort by votes.
15–50Discuss in roundsMove the top topic to Discussing and start the timer. When it ends, vote ↑ keep going / → wrap up / ↓ move on. A majority ↑ adds time.
—After each topicCapture the key takeaway on a green sticky, then move the topic to Discussed.
50–57Takeaways & actionsReview the Discussed column. Turn takeaways that need follow-up into actions with an owner.
57–60CloseQuick feedback on the session. Unfinished topics can go to the next Lean Coffee.

3Using each part of the board

What each area is for, and what to watch for as a facilitator.

1 · To Discuss

  • One topic per sticky, short enough to read from a distance
  • Sort by votes, most at the top
  • Merge duplicates before voting

2 · Discussing

  • Only one topic at a time
  • Tick each timebox round so the group can see time used
  • Note the key takeaway before moving on

3 · Discussed

  • Every topic here gets a green takeaway sticky
  • This is the meeting's record, so photograph it

Keep-going vote

  • Thumbs up, sideways or down works as well as the board icons
  • Majority ↑ extends the timer; otherwise move on
  • Rounds get shorter: 8, then 4, then 2 minutes

How it works row

  • Point to it at the start so newcomers know the flow
  • It keeps the group self-facilitating

Takeaways & Actions

  • Only for things that need follow-up
  • Each action needs an owner and a done when

4Facilitation tips

Hold the timeboxThe timer is what makes Lean Coffee work. Stop when it goes off, even mid-sentence, and vote.
Let the group decideYour job is the process, not the content. Don't steer which topics win.
Shrink roundsShortening the extensions (8, 4, 2) stops one topic taking over the meeting.
Unfinished is fineNot every topic gets covered. Low-vote topics can be handled offline or at the next session.
Remote works wellUse a digital board with voting and a shared timer. Ask people to use reactions for the keep-going vote.
Make it a habitA regular Lean Coffee, such as monthly for a community of practice, builds trust and surfaces issues early.
Common pitfalls: skipping the timer; one person proposing most of the topics; letting the facilitator's agenda creep in; no takeaways captured, so the discussion is lost; groups too large for everyone to speak.

5After the session

Straight away

Photograph the board. Share the Discussed column and the actions with participants.

Actions

Track actions where the group will see them, such as the team board or community page.

Unfinished topics

Carry high-vote topics that weren't reached to the top of the next session's To Discuss column.

Next session

Start by reviewing last time's actions in two minutes, then brainstorm.

6How it fits the toolkit

Lean Coffee is a flexible facilitate tool. Use it to surface topics before a Marginal Gains Workshop, or as a lightweight alternative to a retrospective when the team wants an open conversation.

Create an editable whiteboard for “Lean Coffee”, a workshop board.

Purpose: A structured, agenda-less meeting: the group sets the topics, votes on them, and talks about what matters most.

Layout: a wide landscape board (about 16:11). Make one frame per section below, placed as described. In each frame, add the section name as a heading, then its content as short text labels or empty fields to fill in. Leave space in each frame for sticky notes. Use the header colour given. Keep the wording as written and don't add extra content.
Key: Yellow Sticky = Discussion Topic · Green Sticky = Takeaway / Action · Dot = Vote (3 per person)

Sections:
1. To Discuss: middle left, about 35% wide and 70% tall; header colour blue.
   Content: What do we want to talk about? · One topic per sticky, pitched in a sentence · Dot-vote, then sort by votes (most at the top)
2. Discussing: middle centre, about 25% wide and 70% tall; header colour orange.
   Subtitle: Now
   Content: What are we discussing now? · Current topic: · Place the top-voted topic here · Timebox (tick each round): · 8 min · + 4 min · + 2 min · WHEN THE TIMER ENDS, VOTE · ↑ · → · ↓ · Keep going · Wrap up · Move on · Majority ↑ extends the timer · otherwise pull the next topic · Key takeaway:
3. Discussed: middle right, about 35% wide and 70% tall; header colour emerald green.
   Content: What have we covered? · Move the topic here when the group moves on · Add a green sticky with the key takeaway
