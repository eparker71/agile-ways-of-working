Print Pack

Every board and poster as a print-ready A3 landscape PDF (154 in all), grouped like the home page. They print well on A3 or US 11×17 (Tabloid) paper, and scale up for wall posters: see how to print.

How to print

UsePaperPrinter settings
Team boardA3, or 11×17 in (Tabloid / Ledger)Landscape. A3: actual size. 11×17: Fit to page (about 94%, with narrow side margins).
Wall posterA2 or A1, or 18×24 / 24×36 inSend the PDF to a print shop and ask for it scaled to fit. The PDFs are vector, so they stay sharp at any size.
Personal copyA4, or US LetterLandscape, Fit to page. Readable for reference, but small for writing on.

A sizes share the same shape, so the A3 PDF scales exactly to A4, A2 or A1. US sizes are a different shape, so expect some margin: at the sides on 11×17 and 24×36, at the top and bottom on Letter and 18×24. The cheat sheets print from their own pages: use A3, or 11×17 with Fit to page.
